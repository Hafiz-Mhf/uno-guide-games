1. The Objective

Be the first player to play all the cards in your hand by matching the top card of the Discard pile by Color, Number, or Symbol.

2

2. How a Turn Works

Look at the card on top of the Discard Pile. On your turn, you must match it by:

  • ● Color (e.g. Red on Red)
  • ● Number or Symbol (e.g. 7 on 7, or Skip on Skip)
  • ● Color Card / Wild (allows you to choose among 4 colors anytime!)
3

3. No Match? Draw 1 Card & Pass

If you don't have a matching card in hand, simply draw 1 card from the deck. If that card cannot be played right away, your turn ends there—no continuous drawing until you find a match!

4

4. Shout 'UNO!' at 1 Card

Digital Rounds: Hit that on-screen 'UNO' button before placing down your second-to-last card. Physical Rounds: Shout 'UNO!' loud and proud the second you're down to your last card! Penalty: Draw 2 cards if caught slipping before the next player plays.

5

5. Winning the Round

The first player to discard their final card wins the round! (Yes, winning on an Action Card is 100% legal!)

Tournament House Rules & Etiquette

7 friendly rules to keep our casual championship fun, fair, and flowing smoothly.

01

1. Don't Forget to Call 'UNO!' 🗣️

Draw 2 if Caught

Digital Rounds: Hit that on-screen 'UNO' button before placing down your second-to-last card. Physical Rounds: Shout 'UNO!' loud and proud the second you're down to your last card! Penalty: If someone catches you slipping before the next person plays, you'll have to draw 2 penalty cards. Eyes peeled, everyone!

02

2. No Playable Card? Draw One & Pass 🃏

No Continuous Draw

If you don't have a matching card in hand, simply draw one card from the deck. If that card can't be played right away, your turn ends there—no continuous drawing until you find a match!

03

3. One Card at a Time (No Multiple Drops) ✋

Single Drop Only

Got two Blue 7s or three Yellow 4s? Nice hand, but you can only play one card per turn! No dropping matching sets or multiples at the same time.

04

4. No Stacking (+2 and +4 Stay With You) 🛑

Strictly No Stacking

Stacking is strictly off the table—you can't slap a +2 onto another +2 (or a +4 onto a +4) to pass the pain to your neighbor. If it lands on you, take the hit, draw your cards, and skip your turn gracefully!

05

5. Take Your Time, But Keep It Flowing ⏳

Friendly Pacing

No stressful timers here! Feel free to think through your master plan and strategize. Just keep the game moving so everyone finishes on schedule—our organizers will only give a friendly nudge if a turn takes a bit too long.

06

6. Yes, You Can Finish on an Action Card! 🎉

100% Legal Win

Want to end the game on a high note with a Skip, Reverse, +2, or Color Card / Wild? Go right ahead! Winning on an Action card is 100% legal here.

07

7. Respect the Table Captains & Referees 🤝

Final Ruling

Every physical table in Round 2 will have a designated Table Captain to keep things running smooth and fair. If any playful arguments break out, their word (or the Tournament Director's) is final!

Card Types & Special Mechanics

Special effects in play during Round 1 (1v1 Digital) vs Rounds 2 & 3 (Multi-Player Physical).

🔢

Number Cards (0–9)

Standard Play

Standard colored cards (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) played by matching color or number.

Match Rule: Match active top card by color or face number.
🚫

Skip Card

Turn Action

Rounds 2 & 3: Skips next player's turn. Round 1 (1v1): Skips opponent; take another turn immediately!

⚡ 1v1 Round 1: Opponent skipped; play again immediately!
🔁

Reverse Card

Direction Action

Rounds 2 & 3: Reverses play direction. Round 1 (1v1): Acts as a Skip card; take another turn immediately!

⚡ 1v1 Round 1: Acts as Skip; play again immediately!
➕2️⃣

Draw 2 (+2)

Penalty Action

Next player must draw 2 cards and forfeit their turn (No stacking allowed).

⚠️ House Rule: Draw 2 & lose turn. Strictly no stacking.
🌈

Color Card / Wild

Anytime Play

Can be played on any color or number anytime. Player announces the next active color.

🎨 Color Choice: Choose Red, Yellow, Green, or Blue.
🔥4️⃣

Color +4 / Wild Draw 4

Penalty Action

Player chooses next color; next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn (No stacking).

⚠️ House Rule: Draw 4, lose turn & choose color. No stacking!
