Rule 2: Strict No-Stacking Policy (+2 / +4)

Card stacking is strictly prohibited! You CANNOT place a +2 on a +2, or a +4 on a +4 to pass penalties. If hit with a Draw card, you must draw the cards and forfeit your turn immediately.

🗣️

Rule 1: Calling 'UNO!' (Strict 2-Card Penalty)

You must call out 'UNO!' loudly (or press the digital UNO button) the instant you have only 1 card left. If an opponent or Table Captain catches you with 1 card before the next player starts their turn, you must draw 2 penalty cards.

⏱️

Rule 3: 30-Second Turn Clock

In Round 1 digital lobbies, turns exceeding 30 seconds are subject to automatic timeout. Table Captains may forfeit inactive players to ensure schedule integrity.

🎯

Rule 4: Winning on an Action Card

You are fully permitted to win the game by playing an Action Card (+2, Skip, Reverse, Color Card, or Color +4) as your final card.

⚖️

Rule 5: Table Captain & Referee Decisions Final

Table Captains preside over each physical table in Rounds 2 & 3. Any dispute regarding card drop timing, verbal shouts, or penalties is decided exclusively by the Table Captain.

1. The Objective

Be the first player to empty all cards from your hand before your opponents do.

2

2. How a Turn Works

Look at the card on top of the Discard Pile. On your turn, you must match it by:

  • ● Color (e.g. Red on Red)
  • ● Number or Symbol (e.g. 7 on 7, or Skip on Skip)
  • ● Color Card (allows you to choose among 4 colors anytime!)
3

3. No Match? Draw 1 Card

If you cannot play a matching card, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile. If the card drawn is playable, you can play it immediately!

4

4. Shout 'UNO!' at 1 Card

The moment you play your second-to-last card and hold only 1 card, you MUST shout 'UNO!' (or tap the UNO button). If an opponent catches you before the next player takes a turn, you must draw a 2-card penalty!

5

5. Winning the Round

The first person to discard their final card wins the round!
