# Official UNO Rules & Card Reference | Peraturan Rasmi UNO

## 1. Core Objective | Objektif Utama
- **EN:** Be the first player to empty your hand of all cards before your opponents.
- **BM:** Menjadi pemain pertama yang menghabiskan semua kad di tangan sebelum pihak lawan.

---

## 2. Card Action Mechanics | Mekanik Kad Aksi

| Card / Kad | Type & Visual | Effect in Multi-Player (Rounds 2 & 3) | Effect in 1v1 Digital (Round 1) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skip** 🚫 | Action (Colored) | Skips the next player's entire turn. *(Giliran pemain seterusnya terbatal).* | Skips opponent; you take another turn immediately. *(Lawan diskip; anda jalan sekali lagi).* |
| **Reverse** 🔁 | Action (Colored) | Reverses the direction of play clockwise/counter-clockwise. *(Menukar arah putaran giliran).* | Acts exactly like a **Skip**; you take another turn immediately. *(Berfungsi seperti Skip; anda jalan sekali lagi).* |
| **Draw 2 (+2)** ➕2️⃣ | Action (Colored) | Next player draws 2 cards and forfeits their turn. *(Pemain seterusnya ambil 2 kad & hilang giliran).* | Opponent draws 2 cards and forfeits their turn. *(Lawan ambil 2 kad & hilang giliran).* |
| **Wild** 🌈 | Wild | Player selects the active color for subsequent play. *(Pemain memilih warna seterusnya).* | Player selects the active color for subsequent play. *(Pemain memilih warna seterusnya).* |
| **Wild Draw 4 (+4)** 🔥 | Wild Action | Player selects the active color; next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn. *(Pilih warna; pemain seterusnya ambil 4 kad & hilang giliran).* | Opponent draws 4 cards, loses turn; player selects active color. *(Lawan ambil 4 kad & hilang giliran; anda pilih warna).* |

## 3. Official Tournament House Rules | Peraturan Khas Kejohanan

### Rule 1: The "UNO!" Call | Laungan Wajib "UNO!"
- **Digital Play (Round 1):** You must click the on-screen **UNO** button before playing your second-to-last card.
- **Physical Play (Rounds 2 & 3):** You must verbally announce **"UNO!"** loud and clear the moment you hold only **1 card** in hand.
- **Penalty (Denda):** If another player catches you not saying "UNO!" before the next player takes their turn, you must draw **2 penalty cards** from the deck.

### Rule 2: Strict No-Stacking Policy | Tiada 'Stacking' Kad (+2 / +4)
- **EN:** Stacking is strictly prohibited. You cannot counter a `+2` with another `+2`, nor a `+4` with another `+4`. The affected player must draw the cards and forfeit their turn immediately.
- **BM:** Penindanan kad tidak dibenarkan. Anda tidak boleh menepis kad `+2` dengan `+2` yang lain atau `+4` dengan `+4`. Pemain yang terkena denda wajib mengambil kad dan hilang giliran serta-merta.

### Rule 3: 30-Second Turn Clock | Had Masa Giliran (30 Saat)
- **EN:** In Round 1 digital lobbies, inactive turns exceeding 30 seconds are subject to automatic timeout or forfeit by tournament referees to ensure the schedule runs on time.
- **BM:** Dalam pusingan digital, masa maksimum setiap giliran ialah 30 saat bagi memastikan kelancaran kejohanan mengikut jadual.

### Rule 4: Action Card Finish | Menamatkan Permainan dengan Kad Aksi
- **EN:** A player is fully permitted to win the round by playing an Action Card (`+2`, `Skip`, `Reverse`, or `Wild` / `Wild Draw 4`) as their final card.
- **BM:** Pemain dibenarkan untuk menang pusingan dengan meletakkan Kad Aksi (`+2`, `Skip`, `Reverse`, `Wild`, atau `+4`) sebagai kad terakhir mereka.

### Rule 5: Referee & Table Captain Authority | Keputusan Pengadil
- **EN:** Any card timing disputes or ambiguities during physical table rounds will be decided by the assigned Table Captain. All decisions are final.
- **BM:** Sebarang pertikaian atau kekeliruan semasa pusingan kad fizikal akan diputuskan oleh Ketua Meja (*Table Captain*). Keputusan adalah muktamad.
