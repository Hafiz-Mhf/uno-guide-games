## 2. Round 2 Contingency & Second Chance Wildcard System

### Minimum 4-Player Table Guarantee (16 Attendees)
- If only the minimum of **16 players** attend:
  - Round 1 produces **8 direct match winners**.
  - Naturally distributed across 3 tables, this leaves only 3, 3, and 2 players per table.
  - Playing UNO with only 2 or 3 players per physical table lacks group engagement.
- **The Second Chance Solution:**
  - Tournament Organizers can manually add **Second Chance Wildcards** (chosen from checked-in attendees who lost in Round 1 or are in attendance) directly to Table Alpha, Beta, or Gamma until each table has **at least 4 players**.
  - Second Chance players receive an official purple badge (`🃏 Second Chance` / `🃏 Peluang Kedua`).
  - Second Chance players compete on equal footing on the physical table and can be crowned **Heat Champion** to advance to the Grand Finale.

## 3. Stage Details & Rules

### Stage 2: 🧪 Test Round (3:12 PM – 3:25 PM)
- Zero-stakes device check to ensure all smartphone and laptop browsers can join BuddyBoardGames without firewall or ad issues.
- Quick launcher generates test rooms `HR-Test-01` through `HR-Test-17`.

### Stage 3: Round 1 Digital 1v1 Matches (3:25 PM – 3:45 PM)
- Up to 17 simultaneous 1v1 matches across rooms `HR-Match-01` through `HR-Match-17`.
- Match format: Best of 1 game.
- Winners are recorded at the Tournament Desk and advance to Round 2.

### Stage 4: Round 2 Physical Heats (3:45 PM – 4:05 PM)
- 3 physical tables with standard UNO decks:
  - **Table Alpha (Heat 1):** Min 4 pax
  - **Table Beta (Heat 2):** Min 4 pax
  - **Table Gamma (Heat 3):** Min 4 pax
- Dedicated Table Captains oversee fair play and verify the "UNO!" shout.
- First player to shed all cards wins their heat.

### Stage 5: Round 3 Grand Finale Showcase (4:05 PM – 4:20 PM)
- The 3 heat winners face off at the central showcase table:
  - **🥇 1st Place (Grand Champion):** First to empty hand.
  - **🥈 2nd Place (Runner-Up):** Second to empty hand.
  - **🥉 3rd Place (2nd Runner-Up):** Third finalist.

---

## 4. Tiebreaker & Dispute Resolution
- **Simultaneous Empty Hand:** If two players discard their final card in the same sequence, remaining penalty counts are audited by the Table Captain.
- **Dispute Escalation:** Table Captain rulings are final. Unresolved matters escalate to the Lead Tournament Director.
